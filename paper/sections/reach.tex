\section{Coding the Record}\label{sec:reach}

A benchmark can be missing from a model card because it was irrelevant, never run, or run and withheld. Only the third is disclosure in the sense of \citet{grossman1981}, \citet{milgrom1981} and \citet{dye1985}, and only it is a reader's to price. The within-family drop narrows it: a provider that reported $b$ at $t$ and omits it at $t+1$, an independent score present, has established relevance and possession of the harness by its own earlier behaviour, and the independent score supplies the counterfactual that clinical outcome-reporting takes from registries \citep{chan2004,dwan2013,kirkham2010}. It does not establish that $b$ was run for $t+1$, so the \citet{dye1985} uncertainty narrows rather than closes. The design's ceiling, 37 qualifying pairs, and its detection threshold, roughly 38 points at a single drop, are set out in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}.

The record is now coded. A single coder read the release-time document of each of the 108 releases with a locatable artifact, recording every reported benchmark together with variants, bare mentions and stated benign absences; the ORBIT categories were derived mechanically from those records and the family history, 1,280 cells, of which 31 fall outside the pinned worklist and are excluded. An independent second coder recoded a provider-stratified fifth of the releases, blind to the first coding and with no calibration contact. By Cohen's $\kappa$ \citep{cohen1960} the derived cells agree at $0.95$ $[0.80, 1.00]$ on the judgment the coder actually makes, reported versus not, at $0.85$ $[0.72, 0.95]$ over the full nine categories and at $0.81$ $[0.48, 1.00]$ on the high-versus-low-suspicion split, with intervals from resampling releases rather than cells. Among eligible coded cells 76.9\% report no number (release-level mean 73.1\% $[65.9, 79.2]$), a descriptive rate carrying no evidence of selection by itself.

The disclosed-versus-omitted comparison the coding exists for returns $+8.7$ points $[4.1, 13.9]$, and its identifying counterpart, omitted-eligible cells against postdating ones, returns $+11.4$ $[5.6, 17.0]$, significant against zero at $p = 0.003$. Read against zero that is a finding of concealment. Read against Section~\ref{sec:core} it is the artifact: the same contrast computed with no labels at all sits at $+12.23$, and under every change of measure and window the coded gap lands within 1.3 points of its label-free counterpart. The naive reading is robust, which is the trap: the gap survives two-way provider-and-benchmark errors, reclassifying every variant cell as an omission, and leaving out any provider or benchmark, because what it measures is availability. What survives the correction instead is composition. Holding each release's disclosed count fixed and permuting which benchmarks carry the numbers puts the observed $+8.7$ past all 2,000 draws ($p<10^{-4}$, null standard deviation 1.9): providers print the benchmarks on which they stand well, a selection the artifact cannot manufacture. The drop estimator realises 16 drops across 15 releases and returns $+2.9$ with a sign-flip $p$ of 0.25 over five providers, the underpowered null its calibration priced in advance.
