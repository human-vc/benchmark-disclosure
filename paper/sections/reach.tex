\section{Coding the Record}\label{sec:reach}

A benchmark can be missing from a model card because it was irrelevant, never run, or run and withheld. Only the third is disclosure in the sense of \citet{grossman1981}, \citet{milgrom1981} and \citet{dye1985}, and only it is a reader's to price. The within-family drop narrows it: a provider that reported $b$ at $t$ and omits it at $t+1$, an independent score present, has established relevance and possession of the harness by its own earlier behaviour, with the independent score as the counterfactual clinical outcome-reporting takes from registries \citep{chan2004,dwan2013,kirkham2010}. It does not establish that $b$ was run for $t+1$, so the \citet{dye1985} uncertainty narrows rather than closes. The design's ceiling, 37 qualifying pairs, and its detection threshold, roughly 38 points at one drop, sit in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}.

The record is now coded. A single coder read the release-time document of each of the 100 releases for which one is locatable, eight of the queued 108 having none, recording every reported benchmark with variants, bare mentions and stated benign absences; the ORBIT categories were derived mechanically from those records and the family history, 1,280 cells, 31 of them from five releases outside the pinned worklist and excluded. An independent second coder recoded a provider-stratified fifth, blind to the first coding and with no calibration contact; agreement, Cohen's $\kappa$ on the derived cells \citep{cohen1960} with intervals from resampling releases, is $0.95$ $[0.80, 1.00]$ on the judgment the coder actually makes, reported versus not, $0.85$ $[0.72, 0.95]$ over the full nine categories and $0.81$ $[0.48, 1.00]$ on the high-versus-low-suspicion split; the one E cell each derives is the same cell, and Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting} reports the confusion structure. Among eligible coded cells 76.9\% report no number (release level 73.1\% $[65.9, 79.2]$), a descriptive rate that is no evidence of selection by itself.

The disclosed-versus-omitted comparison returns $+8.7$ points $[4.1, 13.9]$; its identifying counterpart, omitted-eligible cells against postdating ones, returns $+11.4$ $[5.6, 17.0]$, significant against zero at $p = 0.003$. Read against zero that is a finding of concealment. Read against Section~\ref{sec:core} it is the artifact: the same contrast without labels sits at $+12.23$, and under every change of measure and window the coded gap lands within 1.3 points of its label-free counterpart; intervals spanning eleven points leave a strategic component of several points undetected rather than excluded. The naive reading is robust: the gap survives two-way provider-and-benchmark errors, reclassifying every variant cell as an omission, and leaving out any provider or benchmark, because what it measures is availability. What survives instead is composition. Holding each release's disclosed count fixed and permuting which benchmarks carry the numbers puts the observed $+8.7$ past all 2,000 draws ($p<10^{-4}$, null sd 1.9): providers print the benchmarks on which they stand well, a pattern the artifact cannot manufacture, though it does not separate selection on results from targeting of the benchmarks a provider showcases. The drop estimator realises 16 drops across 15 releases and returns $+2.9$ with a sign-flip $p$ of 0.25 over five providers, the underpowered null its calibration priced.
