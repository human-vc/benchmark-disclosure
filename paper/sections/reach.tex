\section{Coding the Record}\label{sec:reach}

A benchmark can be missing from a model card because it was irrelevant, never run, or run and withheld. Only the third is disclosure in the sense of \citet{grossman1981}, \citet{milgrom1981} and \citet{dye1985}, and only it is a reader's to price. The within-family drop narrows it: a provider that reports $b$ at $t$ and omits it at $t+1$, an independent score present, has established relevance and possession of the harness by its own earlier behaviour, with the independent score as the registry-style counterfactual \citep{chan2004,dwan2013,kirkham2010}. It does not establish that $b$ was run for $t+1$, so the \citet{dye1985} uncertainty narrows rather than closes. Its ceiling, 37 pairs, and detection threshold, roughly 38 points at one drop, sit in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}.

A single coder read the release-time document of each of the 100 releases for which one is locatable; eight of the queued 108 have none. The record notes every benchmark a document reports, with variants, bare mentions, and stated benign absences; the ORBIT categories are derived mechanically from these records and the family history. This yields 1,280 coded cells; 31 from five later-vintage releases sit outside the pinned panel and are excluded. A second coder then recoded a provider-stratified fifth of the releases, blind to the first coding and without calibration contact; Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting} records the one release where that blindness broke. Agreement is Cohen's $\kappa$ over the derived cells \citep{cohen1960}, with intervals from resampling releases. The two codings agree at $0.95$ $[0.80, 1.00]$ on the primitive judgment of whether a benchmark was reported, at $0.85$ $[0.72, 0.95]$ over the full category set, and at $0.81$ $[0.48, 1.00]$ on the high-versus-low-suspicion split. The single E cell each coding derives is the same cell, encouraging but no reliability estimate for so rare a category, and Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting} reports the confusion structure. Among the 1,249 eligible coded cells that resolve in the panel, 76.9\% report no number, and the release-level mean is 73.1\% $[65.9, 79.2]$. The rate is descriptive and is not, by itself, evidence of selection.

The disclosed-versus-omitted comparison returns $+8.7$ points $[4.1, 13.9]$ over 63 releases; the intended availability contrast, omitted-eligible against postdating cells over the 71 releases holding both, returns $+11.4$ $[5.6, 17.0]$ and would reject a zero null at $p = 0.003$. Read against Section~\ref{sec:core}, it is the availability gap: the label-free contrast on the same releases is $+14.1$, and under every measure and window the coded gap falls 1.9 to 2.7 points short. The shortfall is an exact accounting identity, the average over the same releases of the reported share times the within-release reported-omitted gap, $+2.7$, so the labels change this contrast only through composition. Intervals spanning eleven points leave a strategic component of several points undetected rather than excluded.

What survives is composition. Reported benchmarks stand 8.7 points above omitted eligible ones within release, a gap larger than all 9,999 count-preserving random assignments ($p = 0.0001$, the draws' floor; null standard deviation 1.9) and one the availability gap cannot produce. It survives coding-error, variant-reclassification, and leave-one-out exercises because the availability gap does; benchmark identity does not carry it, since fixed effects for benchmark beside the release effects raise the coefficient from $+7.4$ to $+9.3$ and conditioning on the window share leaves $+7.4$. It does not identify whether standing drove the reporting: relevance, salience, and within-eligible contamination can move disclosure and performance together.

Possession is the remaining distance; four cuts return one verdict. The drop estimator realises 16 drops across 15 releases and returns $+2.9$, opposite the withholding prediction, sign-flip $p = 0.25$ over five providers, the underpowered null its calibration priced. No document announces an evaluation and withholds its number, across the 295 cells documenting a run. A change-based test, each dropped benchmark against its standing under the previous release, gives $-3.7$, the sign withholding predicts, at permutation $p = 0.16$ over 42 transitions; tiering the deficit by possession evidence and searching for equal-sized substitutions detect nothing further. Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting} details these and three unrepaired limits: unlocatable documents, coverage that may follow provider emphasis, and the best-across-scaffolds rule.
