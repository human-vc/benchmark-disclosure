\section{Coding the Record}\label{sec:reach}

A benchmark can be missing from a model card because it was irrelevant, never run, or run and withheld. Only the third is disclosure in the sense of \citet{grossman1981}, \citet{milgrom1981} and \citet{dye1985}, and only it is a reader's to price. The within-family drop narrows it: a provider that reports $b$ at $t$ and omits it at $t+1$, an independent score present, has established relevance and possession of the harness by its own earlier behaviour, the independent score supplying the counterfactual that registries supply in clinical outcome-reporting \citep{chan2004,dwan2013,kirkham2010}. It does not establish that $b$ was run for $t+1$, so the \citet{dye1985} uncertainty narrows rather than closes. Its ceiling, 37 pairs, and detection threshold, roughly 38 points at one drop, sit in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}.

The record is now coded. A single coder read the release-time document of each of the 100 releases for which one is locatable, eight of the queued 108 having none, recording every reported benchmark with variants, bare mentions, and stated benign absences; the ORBIT categories were derived mechanically from those records and family history, 1,280 cells, 31 excluded with the five later-vintage releases outside the pinned panel. An independent second coder recoded a provider-stratified fifth, blind to the first coding, no calibration contact, one exposed release recorded in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}; agreement, Cohen's $\kappa$ on the derived cells \citep{cohen1960} with intervals from resampling releases, is $0.95$ $[0.80, 1.00]$ on the primitive judgment, reported versus not, $0.85$ $[0.72, 0.95]$ over the full category set and $0.81$ $[0.48, 1.00]$ on the high-versus-low-suspicion split; the single E cell each derives is the same cell (confusion structure in Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting}). Among the 1,249 eligible coded cells resolving in the panel, 76.9\% report no number (release-level 73.1\% $[65.9, 79.2]$), a descriptive rate, no evidence of selection by itself.

\autoref{tab:coding} is the section's result: the disclosed-versus-omitted comparison returns $+8.7$ points $[4.1, 13.9]$ over 63 releases; its identifying counterpart, omitted-eligible against postdating cells over the 71 releases holding both, returns $+11.4$ $[5.6, 17.0]$, significant against zero at $p = 0.003$. Read against zero it would be concealment; read against Section~\ref{sec:core} it is the artifact: computed on the same releases with no labels, the contrast sits at $+14.1$, and under every measure and window the coded gap falls 1.9 to 2.7 points short of its matched counterpart. The shortfall is a point-estimate decomposition, the 23\% reported share times the disclosed-omitted gap, so the labels' entire leverage here is composition; intervals spanning eleven points leave a strategic component of several points undetected rather than excluded. The naive reading is robust to two-way provider-and-benchmark errors, to reclassifying every variant cell as an omission, and to any leave-one-out, because the artifact is. What survives instead is composition. Reported benchmarks stand 8.7 points above omitted eligible ones within release, a composition gap larger than all 9,999 count-preserving random assignments ($p = 0.0001$, the floor the draws allow; null standard deviation 1.9) and one the availability artifact cannot manufacture. Benchmark identity does not carry it: with benchmark fixed effects beside the release effects the coefficient rises from $+7.4$ to $+9.3$, and conditioning on the window share leaves $+7.4$. It does not identify whether standing drove the reporting: relevance, salience, and within-eligible contamination can move disclosure and performance together. The drop estimator realises 16 drops across 15 releases and returns $+2.9$, sign-flip $p = 0.25$ over five providers, the underpowered null its calibration priced. Appendix~\ref{sec:supporting} adds three unrepaired limits: unlocatable documents, coverage that may follow provider emphasis, and the best-across-scaffolds rule.
