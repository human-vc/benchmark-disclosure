\section{Supporting Diagnostics}\label{sec:supporting}

\paragraph{Is window composition a provider's choice?}
Section~\ref{sec:core-recentring} conditions on window composition and notes that doing so is
defensible only if composition is settled before a provider decides what to report. Regressing the
share of a cell's window released after the focal model on provider dummies gives an $R^2$ of
0.060 across 46 organisations, against 0.024 for benchmark dummies over 62 benchmarks and 0.036
for the release date alone. Window composition is close to idiosyncratic at the cell level, which
is consistent with its being set by the evaluator's scoring schedule rather than by the provider,
and is not enough to establish it.

\paragraph{Is the evaluator's coverage selective?}
The estimand conditions on the cells the evaluator scores, so coverage is upstream of everything
else. Coverage averages 8.7 benchmarks per release with a standard deviation of 8.0 and a range of
1 to 38. It correlates with a provider's release count at 0.203 and with the release date at 0.265,
and releases from the densest quartile of providers carry 9.6 scored benchmarks against 8.4 for the
rest. Coverage is therefore mildly tilted toward larger and later providers, and nothing in this
paper corrects for that.
